# Phase G part two: Ai builds and sides better than the field

**The roadmap's goal:** Ai builds and sides decks better than the field. It is done when a change Ai suggests comes with a
simulated gain and a confidence interval, and the suggestions are scored against your results.

**Why now.** Part one (`G.md`, 1.1.61–1.1.69) measured a change to a deck by the goldfish, the Mapper, Shootout and your
games. Phase E gave the app a player that searches (`SearchPlayer`, the ladder, the version in use), and Phase F scored
predictions against what happened (`Backtest`). Part two puts them together: a change is played out in simulated matches,
its gain carries an interval, and every suggestion is held, later, to the games it predicted.

**What is not changing.** The engine plays only the scripts the person asked for; an unwritten card is inert, and every
simulated number says how much of both decks was written (part one's rule 3). Simulated games are never the person's games:
they are kept apart, counted apart, and never enter a rate that says "your games". Hidden is hidden: a simulated seat sees
only its own view (`Seen`), as at the table.

---

## 1. What "done" means

| The roadmap's words | Exactly |
|---|---|
| "Tech choices compared by simulated match win" | Two versions of your deck play the same seeded best-of-three matches against the same opponent — seats swapped, the loser choosing, each deck's siding plan applied before games 2 and 3 — and the difference in match win is paired seed by seed, with a 95 % interval, over a number of seed pairs chosen before it runs. Against a field, the difference is weighted by the strategies' shares. |
| "A side deck chosen from the matchup matrix" | Fifteen cards chosen for the field: each candidate's worth against each strategy read from the evidence there is (Shootout's per-card worth, the ledger's by-card rates, the goldfish against that strategy's interruptions), weighted by the strategies' shares, under the Side Deck's legality; each plan drafted by `SidingMath` and checked by `SideCoverage`. The top choices are played out as series when the person asks. |
| "Ratios chosen by goldfish end boards and by the field's interruptions" | A ±1 search over the deck's ratios, each step compared on the same hands (`VersionCompare`) with the field's interruptions in the opponent's hand at their shares (`StressSet.share`, `FieldProfile.Opens`), so a ratio that bricks less against Ash and Droll is worth what the field makes it. |
| "A simulated gain and a confidence interval" | Every suggestion carries what it was measured by (`goldfish`, `series`, `games`), the gain, its interval, the seeds, and the coverage — kept with the proposal (`Expect.kind`, `seed`, `runId`). |
| "The suggestions scored against your results" | For each applied proposal, the games you played after it at its version, against the field, are set beside what it predicted: whether the gain you saw lies in its interval, the Brier score of its "this helps" against what happened, and over every proposal, how often Ai's intervals held. Shown with the proposals and read by Ai. |
| "Any past format replayed for testing" | The field read as of a date is kept (`<data>/field/<date>.json`), and a test can be run as of that date: that day's banlist, the cards released by then, that day's field and shares. |

## 2. What it stands on, and what is missing

**Built:** the goldfish and the Mapper (`Goldfish.run`, `VersionCompare`, `Paired` with its Newcombe interval and McNemar
p, `CoverageGuard`, `Ablation`, `StarterOdds`), the stress suite (`StressSuite.fromField`, `BoardTraits.through`), the field
(`FieldProfile`, `StrategyRatios.consensus`, `FieldShares`), siding (`SidePlan`, `SidingMath.postSide`/`legalAfter`,
`SideCoverage`), the ledger and the match math (`MatchupLedger`, `TestStats.matchWin`, `MatchMath.field`), the players
(`AiMatch`, `MatchTable`, `SearchPlayer`, the ladder's `LadderGate`), the proposals (`Proposal`, `Expect`, `ProposalBook`,
`Proposals.outcome`, `DeckVersions`), banlists by date (`Banlists.asOf`, `DeckRules`, `FieldLegality.asOf`), and F.2's
`Backtest` for scoring said against happened.

**Missing, and where each is built:**

| Gap | Built in |
|---|---|
| Nothing plays a best-of-three between two library decks with siding; `AiMatch` plays one game and `LadderRun` only the bench. | G2.1: `MatchSeries` — seeded, seats swapped, the loser choosing, `postSide` before games 2 and 3, kept as simulated. |
| No match-win difference with an interval from paired matches, and none weighted by the field. | G2.1: `SeriesReading` on `Paired`, weighted by shares, over seed pairs fixed before the run. |
| No coverage gate on a match between real decks. | G2.1: `SeriesCoverage` — each deck's written share by copies, said beside every number, refused below a floor. |
| Comparisons never see the field's interruptions (`MapCache` strips `through`). | G2.2: a field-weighted ask (`CompareAsk.field`) and `RatioSearch`. |
| Nothing picks a side deck. | G2.2: `SideChooser`, its plans drafted and checked. |
| `Expect` has no kind, seed or run; `Proposals.outcome` is pooled and blind to it. | G2.3: `Expect.kind/seed/runId`; `ProposalScore` against the field-weighted games after, with its Brier and interval coverage. |
| The field is kept only as `latest.json`. | G2.3: snapshots by date; `PastFormat` for a test as of a date. |

**The ceiling, said everywhere.** A simulated match is as good as the scripts and the search: battle effects are inert, the
hand traps that are not written are blanks, and the search plays at Fast. A simulated gain is the app's measurement under
those limits, never a promise; G2.3 is how its record is kept honest.

## 3. Rules this part keeps

1. **Paired or nothing.** Two versions are compared on the same seeds, seats and sides, never two separate runs.
2. **Simulated is its own source.** Series results are kept in `<data>/duel/series/` and counted apart; the ledger reads them
   only when the person turns on the simulated source, and never as "your games".
3. **Coverage beside every number.** Your versions below the floor (half of their Main Deck copies played by the engine)
   are refused with why; an opponent below it is played and flagged "mostly blanks", since most fields are unwritten.
4. **The person presses Start.** A series costs minutes to hours; Ai offers it (`match_request`) with the cost said, as
   `fx_request` does, and never starts one.
5. **Never add seeds after a verdict.** A comparison's seeds and stop are fixed before it runs (the ladder's rule).
6. **Stored data outlives versions.** Each new shape goes into `OldDataTest` in the release that writes it.

## 5. G2.1 as built (1.1.80 / v1.4.56)

**Where.** "Compare with…" (the Mapper) measures by **Same hands** or **Simulated matches**. On matches it shows the field —
the other decks of the web the builder's deck is in, each with its share and what the engine plays of it — the two versions'
coverage, how many seed pairs (4, 8 or 16) and the cost, then **Play the matches**. The result fills in as each match ends:
the change's difference in match win with its 95 % range, each opponent's A and B and their difference, the matches played
and the coverage; **Stop** keeps what is played.

**How** (`core/duel/match/series/`):
- `MatchSeries.play`: one best of three — game 1's first player set by the seed pair, the loser of each game going first in the
  next (after a draw, the same player again), each deck's plan for the turn it plays put in by `SidingMath.postSide` before
  games 2 and 3, a game a draw by limit at turn 10 (`MatchReferee.limit` gives it to the one ahead on life points). Each seat
  is the search in use at Fast, guessing the other's hidden cards from the list it faces (`Prior.Decklist`), as in testing.
- `MatchSeries.seeds`: pairs of a seed played twice, each deck going first in game 1 once. The same seed is the same match on
  any device (`SeriesPlayTest`).
- `SeriesOpponent`: an opponent's deck and its own plans against yours, its share, and each version's plans against it (the
  builder's siding for A; B's own when B is another deck, else A's). `MatchSeries.changed` makes a change by card, never by
  passcode.
- `SeriesRunner`: every match not yet in the run, pair by pair, a few at a time (cores − 1, at most four), each kept as it ends.
- `SeriesReading`: each opponent's matches paired by seed and first player into `Paired` (Newcombe's interval, McNemar's p); the
  field's difference weighted by the shares, its variance the weighted sum of the paired variances (a normal approximation).
- `SeriesCoverage`: the Main Deck copies the engine plays (`GoldfishKit.known`). A version under half is refused; an opponent
  under half is played and flagged.
- **Kept**: `<data>/duel/series/<id>.json` (`SeriesRun`, `SeriesCodec`, forgiving), synced and backed up with the duel folder
  (`Series`, `h.series`). Simulated results never enter the ledger or "your games".
- **Ai**: `match_request` opens the comparison filled in on simulated matches — the person presses Play — and `match_results`
  reads them, read-only.
- **Held by** `MatchSeriesTest` (seeds, the loser choosing, first to two, siding before games 2 and 3, changes by card, the
  pairing, the field's weights, the codec), `SeriesPlayTest` (matches played on the bench as a tournament plays them, the same
  seed the same match, a run picked up again plays nothing twice), `OldDataTest.aSeriesRunAsG21WroteItReads`.
- `tools/shoot.sh --page=mapper --mapper=demo --mapper-series=result`.

**Not yet.** Fields from the field profile (`StrategyRatios.consensus`) rather than the web's decks, picking a run up again
after the app is closed, and the side deck and ratios chosen (G2.2).
