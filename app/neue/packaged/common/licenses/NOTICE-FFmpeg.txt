Neue Master Tool records takes of a presentation with FFmpeg
============================================================

Neue Master Tool (MIT) uses FFmpeg to open the camera and to make the video of a take
(Present, "Record a take"). FFmpeg reaches the app through JavaCV and JavaCPP.

  FFmpeg 8.1.2, as built by the bytedeco project (org.bytedeco:ffmpeg:8.1.2-1.5.14,
  without the "-gpl" classifier): built with --enable-version3 and WITHOUT
  --enable-gpl, so licensed under the GNU Lesser General Public License, version 3
  (LGPL-3.0.txt here; it builds on the GNU General Public License, version 3,
  GPL-3.0.txt here). It carries libvpx (BSD), libopus (BSD), libaom (BSD), SVT-AV1 (BSD),
  LAME (LGPL) and OpenH264 (BSD) among others; Neue Master Tool never asks for OpenH264.
  FFmpeg's source: https://ffmpeg.org/download.html
  The build script: https://github.com/bytedeco/javacpp-presets/tree/master/ffmpeg

  JavaCV 1.5.14 and JavaCPP 1.5.14 (https://github.com/bytedeco/javacv), under the
  Apache License 2.0.

Replacing FFmpeg
----------------
FFmpeg's libraries are separate shared libraries (libavcodec, libavformat, libavdevice,
libavutil, libswscale, libswresample, libavfilter and their JNI bridges), carried inside
the jar named ffmpeg-8.1.2-1.5.14-<platform>.jar in the app's folder and unpacked by
JavaCPP into its cache (~/.javacpp/cache) when they are first used. To use FFmpeg
libraries of your own, build the same FFmpeg version with the bytedeco presets (or
compatible libraries), and either replace that jar, or place your libraries in the cache
folder JavaCPP unpacks to, or start the app with -Dorg.bytedeco.javacpp.pathsFirst=true
and your libraries' folder on java.library.path.
