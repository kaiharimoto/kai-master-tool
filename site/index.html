Neue Master Tool

A Yu-Gi-Oh! deck builder and tournament preparation tool for Windows, macOS, Linux and Android, drawn in ink on paper.

Download

What it is for

Neue Master Tool is for players of the Yu-Gi-Oh! Trading Card Game. It helps you build decks from the official card list, plan how to change your deck between games at a tournament, and prepare for events. It works with card data and card images published by YGOPRODeck, and with the decks you make. It is free and open source.

What it does

  • Build decks with every card's art, sorted into groups, checked against the ban list.
  • Plan siding for each matchup, prepare for an event, and drill your plans.
  • An optional assistant that knows the cards and your decks, using a model you connect yourself.
  • Keep your decks the same on every device through a place you already have: a synced folder, a WebDAV server, or your own Google Drive.

How it uses Google

The app uses one Google API, the Google Drive API, for one purpose: when you choose to sync through Google Drive, it keeps a copy of your decks and settings in the app’s own hidden folder in your Drive (the drive.appdata permission), so your laptop, desktop and phone show the same decks. It cannot see any other file in your Drive.

The app does not create, generate or edit images of people, and does not generate images of any kind. It does not use any Google API to make, alter or share intimate or sexual imagery, including AI-generated non-consensual intimate imagery (NCII), and its terms forbid using it for that. The only pictures it handles are Yu-Gi-Oh! card art and pictures you add yourself for your own cards or decklists.

Your data

Everything you make stays on your devices. There are no accounts with us and no servers of ours. When you turn on sync with Google Drive, the app uses only its own hidden folder in your Drive. Read the privacy policy.
